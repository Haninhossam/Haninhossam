I build the data pipelines that AI systems depend on, and I research how to make those systems reliable in health and safety settings. My work spans computer vision, retrieval-augmented generation, biomedical signals, and real-time data platforms. B.Sc. in Computer Science and AI, E-JUST. I am applying to MS programs in Data Science and Engineering.

---

## Research interests

- **Grounded LLM systems:** retrieval-augmented and agentic pipelines that answer from verified sources, especially where an unsupported answer is costly (healthcare).
- **Reliable data infrastructure for health AI:** streaming and batch pipelines for sensor, signal and imaging data.
- **Efficient deep learning for edge deployment:** video and biomedical-signal models that run on constrained hardware.

## Featured projects

### [Real-Time News Sentiment Pipeline](https://github.com/Haninhossam/sentiment-pipeline)
Kafka, PySpark Structured Streaming, DistilBERT, ClickHouse and an 11-panel Grafana dashboard, with a multi-agent layer (LangGraph, CrewAI, Celery) that detects sentiment anomalies and sends LLM-written root-cause analyses to Slack. Fully containerized, no paid APIs.

### [GlowPipe: Skincare Intelligence Platform](https://github.com/NouranMohamed07/GlowPipe)
ITI graduation project, built with a team of five. Batch (Airflow, PySpark, Snowflake) and streaming (Kafka, Spark Structured Streaming) pipelines on a medallion architecture. I built the hybrid recommendation engine (rule-based filtering, weighted scoring, ranking) served through FastAPI.

### [E-Commerce Data Engineering Pipeline](https://github.com/Haninhossam/E-commerce-Data-Engineering-Pipeline)
Modular ETL pipeline orchestrated with Prefect in Dockerized services.
